    Meet StreamUnify

    Find where to watch movies and TV shows

    StreamUnify brings streaming discovery into one place. Search for a movie or TV series, compare its available services in your selected country, and open a provider or title link when available.

    Search across streaming services

    Search by title or actor name to discover movies and TV-series appearances without checking each streaming app separately. Select your services to filter results by the subscriptions you already have.

    Explore free watch options

    Use the Free filter to find titles with reported free availability in your country. Providers may include ads, require an account, or change their catalogs.

    Keep a Premium watchlist

    Save movies and shows to your account with Premium. Choose $2.99 per month or $30 per year; streaming provider subscriptions are separate.

    Save your watchlist

    Sign in to save movies and shows to your account. Unlimited searches and watchlists are included during the free launch period. Streaming provider subscriptions are separate.

    What is StreamUnify?

    StreamUnify, also written as Stream Unify, is a movie and TV streaming search service. It helps you discover what to watch and which streaming providers list it. StreamUnify does not host or play movies and TV shows.

    Which streaming services can I search?

    Free membership includes 10 searches per monthly period across Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Peacock, Sling TV, Max, discovery+, Paramount+, Crunchyroll, and Tubi in the United States. Premium includes unlimited searches across supported services and countries, plus an account watchlist. Coverage varies by title and country.

    During the free launch period, signed-in users can search all supported services and countries without a monthly search limit. Coverage varies by title and country.

    Does Premium include Netflix or other provider subscriptions?

    No. A StreamUnify membership pays for discovery and watchlist features. Watching a title may require a separate subscription, rental, purchase, or account with the streaming provider. Availability and links can change.

    Does free launch access include streaming provider subscriptions?

    No. Free launch access includes StreamUnify discovery and watchlist features. Watching a title may require a separate subscription, rental, purchase, or account with the streaming provider. Availability and links can change.
